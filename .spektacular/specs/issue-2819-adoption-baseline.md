  ISO downloads from other assets. Exclude internal adopter entries and the
  ecosystem table. Keep unknown sources unavailable.
- **S2 — Receipt:** Save UTC times, source URLs and the local evidence commit

  not claim a future November snapshot was observed.

  a run summary, with a documented review/publication owner and missing inputs.

  OS telemetry or list new adopters without confirmation.
