# contract

A machine-checked statement of what an artifact must satisfy: e.g. the desktop experience contract (`verify-desktop-experience.sh`, marker on `ttyS0`), the CI contract (`green-criteria.yml` vs workflows, `tests/test_ci_contract.py`), and the tacklebox recipe contract (`docs/TACKLEBOX-CONTRACT.md`).
