## Q4 Adoption Outreach Workflow

Before opening new ecosystem outreach issues:

1. **Check this ledger** for an existing row on the same channel/target
2. **Search docs/README.md** for a draft under similar title
3. **Search closed issues** with `outreach` label for past campaigns on the same target

This prevents duplicate issues and consolidates outreach coordination. See issue #2897 (process discipline) for context.

## Update rules

- Replace **No** only with a date and a durable public evidence link.
- Record no-response and declined outreach without converting it into an
  adopter count.
- Keep production, development/evaluation, and anonymous reports as separate
  measures.
- Do not use stars, downloads, image pulls, CI jobs, or ecosystem rows as
  evidence of organizational adoption.
- Mark rows as "Ready for send" once draft material is verified and Q4 timeline is confirmed, before waiting on human send.
