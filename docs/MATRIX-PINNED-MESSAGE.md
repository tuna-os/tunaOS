## #new-to-tunaos Pinned Message

**Welcome to TunaOS!** 👋

If you're new here, here are the best ways to get started and involved:

1. **Read the Docs:** Check out our official documentation at [tunaos.org](https://tunaos.org) to learn about our bootc-based images, installation, and variants.
2. **Ways to Contribute:** Want to help? Read our [Ways to Contribute post](https://tunaos.org/contribute) for an overview of how to get involved, from testing to packaging.
3. **Good First Issues (GFI):** Looking for a starting point in the codebase? Browse our [GFI label query](https://github.com/tuna-os/tunaOS/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3A%22good+first+issue%22) for accessible tasks.

**Etiquette:**
- Please keep discussions on-topic and respectful (see our [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/tuna-os/tunaOS/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)).
- If you have a support question, checking the docs or GitHub Discussions first is appreciated!

We also host a **monthly office hours** session — keep an eye out for announcements here in the room. Enjoy your stay!
