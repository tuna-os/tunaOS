# Contributor roadmap

Start with [your first PR](CONTRIBUTOR-FIRST-PR.md). This roadmap connects
the starter track to the existing [mentorship ladder](../CONTRIBUTING-ADVANCED.md#mentorship-ladder).
It supports [#3117](https://github.com/tuna-os/tunaos/issues/3117), with no
automatic titles, access grants, or promise of promotion.

| Stage | Work to try | Evidence and next step |
|---|---|---|
| Beginner | One small doc fix, then a test case with a mentor | Explain the change, run checks, and answer review. Choose a second task in the same area. |
| Intermediate | Bug fixes, small features, deeper guides; build a history over six or more weeks | Reproduce a bug, test the fix, and review another contributor's work with their agreement. Time alone is not a qualification. |
| Maintainer candidate | Help with roadmap work, variant proposals, and ecosystem work | Show sustained review and support work. Ask the current maintainer about responsibilities and access through the existing project process. |

New variants still follow [VARIANT-LIFECYCLE](../VARIANT-LIFECYCLE.md), and
design changes still follow [RFC-PROCESS](../RFC-PROCESS.md). The
[repository map](../CONTRIBUTING-ADVANCED.md#find-the-right-repository)
helps you find the right producer and reviewer.

## Weekly help and mentorship

The [weekly triage](../CONTRIBUTING.md#weekly-contributor-triage) remains the
async help path. Review unanswered claims and PRs first, then match mentor
requests and replenish the five-task track. Record the responder and next
check-in on each issue after the person accepts.

Issue #3117 proposes a one-hour Thursday session. That live session is
**not scheduled**. Before any announcement, a volunteer host must confirm
the UTC time, start date, capacity, and public calendar URL on #3117. Follow
the existing [office-hours process](../CONTRIBUTING-ADVANCED.md#office-hours-and-asynchronous-questions).
The program has no new account or calendar.

Proposed one-hour agenda: 20 minutes for first-PR questions, 20 for review,
and 20 for blockers and task selection. Attendance in Matrix is optional.
After a session, post the date, issue/PR links, decisions, next actions, and
accepted owners on #3117. Record an attendance count only with consent;
do not publish a list of attendees without their permission.

## Recognition and retention

Use [CONTRIBUTORS](../CONTRIBUTORS.md) for public thanks backed by merged
PRs. At monthly triage, invite two or three contributors to a Community
Highlight. Ask for their preferred name, handle, summary, and consent to
publication. Each highlight should link the merged work, explain its effect,
and invite the next contribution. A person can decline or use only a handle.

The docs-site publisher reviews and publishes the highlight in
[`tuna-os/docs`](https://github.com/tuna-os/docs). A draft in this repository
is not a published blog post. Record the published URL on #3117 and in the
monthly snapshot. A publisher must accept a date before anyone can announce it.
Rotate invitations among new and repeat authors.

## Measure outcomes

Use the monthly window and human-verification rules in
[ADOPTION-METRICS](../ADOPTION-METRICS.md). On #3117, link claims, responses,
merged PRs, accepted pairings, repeat contributions, and published highlights.
Report unavailable data as unknown. Keep these program goals separate from
results:

- Within eight weeks of the first accepted claim: five humans from outside the project with activity.
  Count each person with a claim, review, or PR once.
- During Q4: three merged PRs from new humans.
  Also measure the adoption-plan goal: five merged PRs from three humans with one repeat.
- After live sessions begin: two people besides the maintainer attend twice.
  Measure the count with consent across two sessions.
- Track stars over the eight-week window against the issue's goal of 20 new stars.
  Do not claim the program caused the change.
- Link P1 issues that contributor PRs resolve and the evidence for closure.

Human contributions are not proof of OS use. Adopter entries still need the
separate [consent workflow](ADOPTION-CALL.md). Document contributions in
satellite repositories as such. They do not prove a rise in core maintainer capacity.
